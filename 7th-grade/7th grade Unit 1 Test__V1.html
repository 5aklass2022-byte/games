A Plan That Worked

At the beginning of the school year, Victor felt worried because he was often late and forgot his homework. His sister advised him to make a weekly plan. Victor wrote down his lessons, football practice and free time. He also decided to go to bed earlier. Now he usually finishes his homework before dinner. This week he is preparing a presentation about online learning with his classmates. He has never made a presentation with them before, but they are enjoying the project. Last Friday, Victor visited the school library and found two useful books. He thinks that planning helps him learn better and still have time for his friends.

V. Writing — 15 points

Write an email to a friend about how you organise your school week. Write 70–90 words. Describe TWO activities, explain TWO reasons why planning helps you, and give TWO examples from your week.

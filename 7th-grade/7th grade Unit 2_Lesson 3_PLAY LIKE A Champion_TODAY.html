📘 Grammar · PLAY / GO / DO

⚽ PLAY
ball games & competitive games
play tennis, play football
🏊 GO
many -ing activities
go swimming, go cycling
🥋 DO
exercise, martial arts, athletics
do boxing, do karate, do athletics

PLAY + games with a ball; GO + many sports ending in -ing; DO + exercises and some sports. Exception: DO boxing.

Remember: He plays tennis, but he plays the guitar.

I play tennis. She goes swimming. They do karate. He plays the guitar.

Your task: Write 70–90 words about the Highland Games. Include: (1) where they take place; (2) when they are held; (3) the name of the Scottish skirt; (4) two traditional sports; (5) one extra fact you have researched. If you cannot research now, mark the last point as “to research”.

🧰 Useful words & phrases

Highland Games / Scotland / Scottish / traditional / take place / during the summer / kilt / tug-of-war / tossing the caber / bagpipes / drummers / strong athletes / in my opinion / interesting tradition

0 words

The Highland Games are traditional sporting events in Scotland. They usually take place during the warmer months in different parts of the country. People often wear kilts, traditional Scottish skirts. Visitors can watch sports such as tug-of-war and tossing the caber. Music is important too: bagpipers and drummers create a special atmosphere. I think the Highland Games are exciting because they bring people together and celebrate Scottish culture. I would like to watch these games one day. For the additional researched fact, consult a reliable source.

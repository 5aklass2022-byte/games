Textbook exercises 12–13

🤝 Making suggestions

Let's + base verb: Let's draw a picture together.
How about + -ing: How about riding our bikes?
Accept: What a great idea! Decline: Sorry, I can't.

Ion: I like playing video games after school. How about you, Doina?

Doina: Actually, I love playing video games too.

Ion: Let's play Counterstrike together.

Doina: What a great idea!

Emil: Well, I love riding my bike after school. And you, Laura?

Laura: Me too. I love it.

Emil: How about riding together today after school?

Laura: Sorry I can't! I have to go to the piano lessons instead.

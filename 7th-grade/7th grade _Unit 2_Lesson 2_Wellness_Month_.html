“You must be the change you want to see in the world.” — Mahatma Gandhi (as printed in the textbook)
I agree with this quote because change begins with our own actions. If we want a healthier world, we must start with ourselves. For example, I must eat healthier food and exercise more. I can also encourage my friends to make good choices. Small steps can make a big difference.

Textbook Exercise 13

✍️ Writing · My Typical School Day

Write to a friend about a typical school day in your life (70–90 words). Use Present Simple for your routine; use Present Continuous only for what is happening now.

Checklist: greeting ✓ time expressions ✓ daily routine ✓ correct verb forms ✓ closing ✓

Hi Alex,\nI usually get up at seven o’clock on school days. I have breakfast and walk to school with my friend. Our lessons start at half past eight. I enjoy English because we read stories and work in groups. At lunchtime I talk to my classmates. After school, I do my homework and sometimes play football. I usually go to bed at ten. Today I am writing this message and listening to music.\nWrite back soon!\nNicu

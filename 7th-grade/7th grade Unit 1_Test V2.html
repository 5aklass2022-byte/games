A School Trip to Remember

Last month, Ana and her classmates travelled to Orheiul Vechi. Their teacher wanted them to learn more about Moldova’s beautiful places. The pupils climbed a hill and took photos of the wonderful view. Ana was excited because she had never visited this place before. They returned to school in the afternoon and shared their pictures. Now Ana is working on a digital poster about the trip. She usually studies in the classroom, but today she is preparing the poster at home. Her brother thinks she should add a map and a few facts about the place. Ana agrees with him. She believes that learning outside the classroom can be interesting and useful.

V. Writing — 15 points

Write an email to a friend about a memorable trip in Moldova. Write 70–90 words. Describe TWO things you did, give TWO reasons why you enjoyed the trip, and include TWO specific examples or details.

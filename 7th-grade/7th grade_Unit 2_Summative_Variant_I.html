A Race That Changed Everything

Last spring, Alex joined a charity race organised by his school. He had trained for six weeks because he wanted to improve his fitness. On the morning of the race, he ate a healthy breakfast and arrived early at the sports ground. At first, Alex ran confidently. After two kilometres, he noticed that his classmate Ben had fallen near a turn. Ben had hurt his ankle and could not stand up. Although Alex wanted to finish among the first runners, he stopped and called a teacher. He stayed with Ben until help arrived. When Alex finally crossed the finish line, most pupils had already finished. He was disappointed for a moment, but the crowd applauded him. The head teacher thanked him for helping another runner. Later, Alex and Ben prepared an infographic about sportsmanship and healthy habits. Alex realised that success was not only about winning.

A Month of Healthy Changes

In October, Maya and three friends took part in their school's Wellness Month. Before the programme began, Maya had often stayed up late and skipped breakfast. She found it difficult to concentrate in class and rarely exercised. The friends decided to make small changes together. They prepared healthy lunches, walked after school and tried to sleep eight hours each night. Maya also started cycling with her brother twice a week. At first, she found the new routine difficult, but her friends encouraged her to continue. After four weeks, Maya felt more energetic and her concentration improved. Her group created an infographic to share the results with classmates. During the presentation, Maya explained that she had changed her habits because she wanted to feel better, not because she wanted to win a prize. Their teacher praised the group for their teamwork and determination.

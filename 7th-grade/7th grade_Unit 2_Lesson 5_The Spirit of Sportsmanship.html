Grammar focus

⏳ Past Perfect — had + Past Participle

Explanation:

Earlier past action

He had finished his homework.

① Past Perfect — first

Later past action

Then he went to play football.

② Past Simple — second

7. Find sentences in the text containing had + past participle. What does this verb form indicate?

“He hadn't done anything special.” · “He had started practising the sport in 2015…” · “He had finished the marathon…” — The past perfect refers to an action before another past moment.

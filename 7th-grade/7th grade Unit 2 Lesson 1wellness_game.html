🧠 Grammar Academy

1. Adjective + -ness → Noun

Rule: Add -ness to many adjectives to name a quality or state.
kind → kindness (bunătate / доброта)
happy → happiness (fericire / счастье): y changes to i.
ill → illness (boală / болезнь).
Some words change spelling: polite → politeness, fit → fitness (double t).
Important: not every noun ending in -ness is uncountable in all contexts.

2. Uncountable Nouns

Rule: Uncountable nouns are not normally counted individually. Use a singular verb: Water is important. This advice is useful. Not an advice or advices; say some advice or a piece of advice.
Concrete: water, food, sugar, salt (physical substances).
Abstract: wellness, happiness, knowledge, information, advice (ideas or qualities).
Use much with uncountable nouns and many with countable nouns: much water / many meals.

Pronunciation corner

The letter sequence isl is pronounced /aɪl/ in these words. The s is silent: isle /aɪl/, island /ˈaɪlənd/, islander /ˈaɪləndə(r)/, aisle /aɪl/.

🧠 Grammar Focus — Indefinite Pronouns

somebody / someone — a person (usually affirmative); anybody / anyone — a person (often questions or negatives); nobody / no one — not a single person; everybody / everyone — all people.

Possessive ’s

Add ’s to indefinite pronouns: somebody’s, anyone’s, nobody’s, everybody’s. Examples: Is this anybody’s backpack? / They were living in somebody’s cottage.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăPronumele nehotărâte somebody, anybody, nobody, everybody indică persoane. Pentru a arăta posesia, adăugăm ’s: somebody’s book = cartea cuiva; nobody’s fault = vina nimănui.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиНеопределённые местоимения somebody, anybody, nobody, everybody обозначают людей. Для выражения принадлежности добавляем ’s: somebody’s book — чья-то книга; nobody’s fault — ничья вина.

Success Starts with Determination 🌟

I believe success means achieving something important through hard work and determination. It is not only about becoming rich or famous. Successful people face difficulties, but they do not give up. Stephen Hawking is a great example. Although he had a serious illness, he continued studying the universe and sharing his ideas with others.

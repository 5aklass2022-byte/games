9 · Writing

Future Leaders Exchange Program (FLEX) · English Access Microscholarship Program · Benjamin Franklin Transatlantic Fellows Summer Institute Program

Model speech (illustrative; verify current programme requirements):

Good morning, everyone! Today I want to tell you why scholarships can change our future. One programme worth researching is FLEX. International programmes can help students improve their English, meet new people and become more independent. Applying takes time, but the process can teach us useful skills. We should read the official eligibility rules, prepare carefully and ask our teachers for advice. Even if we are not selected, we can learn from the experience. I encourage you to explore scholarship opportunities and take the first step towards your goals. Thank you for listening!

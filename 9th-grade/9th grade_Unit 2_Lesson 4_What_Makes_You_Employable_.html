Exercise 10 — Create Your Own CV

📄 How to Create a Professional Resume — Step-by-Step Guide

A CV (curriculum vitae) / resume summarises your skills, education, experience and achievements. A school student can use one when applying for scholarships, internships, volunteering or part-time work. It should be clear, truthful, well-organised and easy to read.

1. Contact information

Your name, a professional email and an appropriate way to contact you. For classroom practice, use fictional contact details; do not publish your home address or phone number.

2. About me / Personal profile

Write 2–3 sentences about your strengths, interests, achievements and goals. Example: I am a motivated secondary-school student who enjoys teamwork and digital projects. I hope to study computer science.

3. Skills

Choose specific abilities that distinguish you: communication, foreign languages, computer skills, problem-solving, leadership or creativity. Give evidence where possible.

4. Education

Write your school, expected graduation year, relevant subjects or courses. GPA is optional and should be included only when relevant.

5. Extracurricular activities & community service

Mention school clubs, projects, volunteering and responsibilities. Describe what you actually did.

6. Awards, honours & achievements

List genuine prizes, certificates, competitions or leadership roles. You may also add hobbies if they demonstrate useful interests.

CV writing tips: Use short phrases and bullet points; start descriptions with action verbs such as organised, created, helped, designed, participated; check spelling; keep a student CV to one page when possible. Never invent qualifications or experience.

Alex Morgan

Contact: alex.student@example.com (fictional)

I am a responsible and creative secondary-school student interested in technology and community projects. I enjoy solving problems and working in teams.

SKILLS

EDUCATION

EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES & COMMUNITY SERVICE

AWARDS & ACHIEVEMENTS

HOBBIES
Reading · Cycling · Digital design

This is a fictional model for learning; pupils should replace it with their own truthful information.

🇷🇴 Cum redactezi un CV: Date de contact → Despre mine (2–3 propoziții) → Abilități → Studii → Activități extracurriculare și voluntariat → Premii și realizări → Hobby-uri (opțional). Folosește informații reale, propoziții clare și o structură aerisită.

🇷🇺 Как составить резюме: Контактные данные → О себе (2–3 предложения) → Навыки → Образование → Внеучебная деятельность и волонтёрство → Награды и достижения → Увлечения (по желанию). Указывайте только достоверные сведения, пишите кратко и структурированно.

📘 Grammar Focus — Present and Past Tenses

Present Simple

Form: I work / She works; Do you work? / She doesn't work.

Use: routines, habits, facts. My father writes letters every morning.

Markers: always, usually, every day.

Present Continuous

Form: am/is/are + verb-ing.

Use: action happening now or temporary situation. It is snowing now.

Markers: now, at the moment, look!

Present Perfect

Form: have/has + past participle.

Use: experience, result now, or a situation continuing to the present. She has earned money for three years.

Markers: for, since, ever, never, already.

Past Simple

Form: past verb form / did + base verb.

Use: completed past event. It rained hard yesterday.

Markers: yesterday, last night, in 2020.

Past Continuous

Form: was/were + verb-ing.

Use: action in progress at a past moment. The children were watching TV when the lights went out.

Past Perfect

Form: had + past participle.

Use: an earlier action before another past action. The train had already left when we arrived.

Compare: I work every day (habit) · I am working now (now) · I have worked here for three years (until now) · I worked yesterday (finished past) · I was working when she called (ongoing past) · I had worked before she arrived (earlier past).
🇷🇴 Explicație

Present Simple exprimă obiceiuri; Present Continuous — acțiuni în desfășurare; Present Perfect — legătura trecutului cu prezentul. Past Simple exprimă acțiuni încheiate; Past Continuous — acțiuni în desfășurare în trecut; Past Perfect — o acțiune anterioară alteia din trecut.

🇷🇺 Объяснение

Present Simple — привычки и факты; Present Continuous — действие сейчас; Present Perfect — прошлое, связанное с настоящим. Past Simple — завершённое действие в прошлом; Past Continuous — процесс в прошлом; Past Perfect — действие, произошедшее раньше другого прошлого события.

Exercise 17 — Writing: Opinion Article (110–120 words)

Statement: People used to respect serious professions, like a lawyer or a doctor. Now they idolise influencers and YouTubers. Provide two examples to support the advantages and/or disadvantages of both types of jobs.

Structure: Introduction and opinion → First argument + example → Second argument + example → Conclusion. Linkers: Firstly, For example, However, Moreover, In conclusion.

Teacher-created model article

Today, many teenagers admire influencers and YouTubers more than doctors or lawyers. I think both traditional and modern professions deserve respect, but for different reasons.

Firstly, doctors and lawyers provide essential services. For example, doctors save lives and help communities stay healthy. Their work requires years of training and great responsibility.

Secondly, content creators can educate and inspire large audiences. A YouTuber who explains science clearly may encourage students to learn new skills. However, some influencers promote unrealistic lifestyles or products they do not understand.

In conclusion, we should judge people by their skills, honesty and contribution to society, not simply by their popularity. Every profession has value when it benefits others.

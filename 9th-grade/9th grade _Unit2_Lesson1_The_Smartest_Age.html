📐 Grammar Focus — Verbal Phrases

Verb/adjective + preposition: protect from, dream about, take advantage of, satisfied with, thankful to someone / for something, excited about, fond of, involved in, look forward to + -ing.

A preposition is part of the phrase: I look forward to meeting you (not to meet).

Phrasal verbs: put on (îmbrăca / надевать), speed up (accelera / ускоряться), run after (alerga după / бежать за), pick up (ridica / подбирать), look down on (disprețui / смотреть свысока), get on (urca / садиться в транспорт).

Exercise 14 · Writing — Extracurricular activities

Task: “I make most of my friends through my extracurricular activities.” Write a 110–120-word paragraph. Give arguments and examples.

✍️ Show model paragraph

Extracurricular activities are an important part of my school life because they help me make friends and learn new skills. Last year, I joined a photography club, where I met students who shared my interests. At first, I was shy, but working on creative projects made it easier to talk to others. We planned exhibitions, helped each other and learned to respect different opinions. Clubs also teach responsibility because everyone has a role in the team. I believe schools should offer more activities, including coding, sports and music. These clubs give teenagers a chance to discover their talents and become more confident. For these reasons, I strongly agree that extracurricular activities are a great way to build friendships.

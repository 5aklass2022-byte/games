⭐ Compound nouns: which word changes?

Compound nouns contain more than one word. Usually pluralize the main noun:

one passer-by → two passers-by · one mother-in-law → two mothers-in-law.

If the compound is one ordinary joined word, pluralize the whole word: toolbox → toolboxes. Some compounds use the usual ending: spoonful → spoonfuls, bus stop → bus stops.

Why: I believe everyone deserves good healthcare, and I enjoy learning about science.

My three steps: First, I will improve my biology and English. Next, I will volunteer when I have the opportunity. Finally, I will research universities and entrance requirements.

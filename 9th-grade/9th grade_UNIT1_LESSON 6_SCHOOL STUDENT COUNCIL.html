9. Writing — Official application letter (110–120 words)

Dear School Administration, I am writing to apply for the position of School Student Council President. I believe I would be a good candidate because I am responsible, friendly, and ready to listen to other students. If elected, I will organise regular meetings to collect students’ ideas. I also want to improve recycling at school and introduce more activities for students to work together. We can create a suggestion box for sharing opinions and plan charity events to help our community. I am confident that, with teamwork and perseverance, we can make our school a better place for everyone. Thank you for considering my application. Yours faithfully, A Student

I. Reading · 10 points

A School Project with a Future

Last autumn, sixteen-year-old Daniel joined a STEM project at his school. His team wanted to solve a real problem: students often left the classroom lights on after lessons. Daniel suggested designing a simple smart system that could switch the lights off automatically. At first, the team disagreed about the best approach. Some students preferred making posters to remind classmates to save energy. However, they finally decided to build a small model together. Their science teacher helped them plan the experiment, while a local engineer explained how sensors work. The students met twice a week and shared different responsibilities. Daniel researched materials, his friend Maria prepared the presentation, and two classmates tested the model. They made several mistakes, but they did not give up. When the model worked, the school invited the team to present it to younger students. Daniel learned that communication and perseverance were as important as technical knowledge. He now hopes to study engineering at university because he wants to create useful technology. He believes that successful projects begin when people listen to one another and work towards a common goal.

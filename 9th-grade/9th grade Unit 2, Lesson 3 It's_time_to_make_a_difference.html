🧩 Language Academy — Confusing Words

Lesson 3 contains confusing words rather than a separate traditional grammar rule. Study their meanings before Exercise 9.

Examples

My friends accept my ideas. · Except for Monday, I am free.

Social media can affect our choices. · The effect was surprising.

Teachers influence students. · The new law had a big impact.

Job interview language

Use Present Simple for general skills: I work well in teams. Use Past Simple for completed experience: I completed a training course last year.

🇷🇴 Present Simple — abilități și fapte generale; Past Simple — experiențe încheiate.
🇷🇺 Present Simple — навыки и общие факты; Past Simple — завершённый опыт.

✍️ Writing Studio — My Dream Career

Textbook note: The supplied pages end with the listening interview. This writing task is an additional practice activity to consolidate the lesson.

Task: Write a short job application paragraph (90–110 words)

Explain the job you want, your skills, your experience or education, and why you would be a good candidate.

  1. Introduce yourself and the job.
  2. Describe your education or interests.
  3. Explain your strengths and skills.
  4. Explain why you want this job.
  5. End politely and positively.

Useful phrases: I am applying for… · I have experience in… · My strengths include… · I would be a good candidate because…

📝 Model text (100 words)
My name is Alex, and I am interested in becoming a social media manager. I enjoy creating digital content and learning how online platforms work. At school, I study English and information technology, and I often help with class projects. I am creative, responsible and good at teamwork. I also enjoy communicating with different people. Although I do not have professional experience yet, I am ready to learn new skills and work hard. I would like this job because it combines technology and creativity. I believe I could make a positive difference in a friendly and ambitious team.

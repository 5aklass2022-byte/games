Comprehension · Answer the textbook questions

1. What can students visit in New York City?

The Empire State Building observatory.

2. What is the aim of such visits?

To learn about engineering, energy efficiency and design.

3. What did a high school senior from Michigan win?

An app challenge with a math game he designed.

Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics.

5. What skills can STEAM students develop?

Soft and hard skills, curiosity, creativity and problem-solving.

To encourage curiosity about science, maths and art.

7. What did Latitude High School students work on?

Building tiny homes for unhoused youth.

8. Who are Purdue Polytechnic High School partners?

The Indianapolis Zoo, Subaru and Eli Lilly and Co.

🧠 Grammar Lab · Either / Neither

Read the explanation in your selected language before Exercise 9.

EITHER = one of two possible choices. Use either when both alternatives are possible. Example: Either book is fine.
NEITHER = not one and not the other. Example: Neither answer is correct.

⚠️ Either ≠ Neither. Choose by meaning, not by word position.

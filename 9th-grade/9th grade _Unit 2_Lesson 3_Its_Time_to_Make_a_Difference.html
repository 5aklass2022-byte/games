📘 Grammar Focus — Countable & Uncountable Nouns

What are they?

Countable nouns name things we can count individually: one job, two jobs; an experience (an event); a paper (a report). They can take a/an in the singular and numbers in the plural.

Uncountable nouns name substances, abstract ideas or activities normally not counted as separate units: iron (metal), glass (material), work (activity), hair (in general), experience (knowledge), time (duration), paper (material). We do not normally say an information or two advices.

Some nouns can be both, with different meanings:

iron: iron is a metal (U) / an iron is an appliance (C).
glass: glass is a material (U) / two glasses are containers (C).
time: I don't have much time (U) / three times (C).
paper: paper as material (U) / a paper as a report (C).
light: light as energy (U) / lights as lamps (C).
work: work as activity (U) / a work of art (C).
hair: hair in general (U) / a hair or individual hairs (C).
experience: experience as skill (U) / an experience as an event (C).

Quantifiers: many / a few + countable plural; much / a little + uncountable; some / a lot of + both.

Test: Can I naturally say one, two, three of this meaning? If yes, it is countable in that context.

🇷🇴 Explicație

Substantivele numărabile pot fi numărate: a job, two jobs. Cele nenumărabile desemnează materiale, activități sau concepte: work, iron, time. Același substantiv poate avea ambele valori, în funcție de sens: glass = sticlă (U), a glass = un pahar (C).

🇷🇺 Объяснение

Исчисляемые существительные можно посчитать: a job, two jobs. Неисчисляемые обозначают вещества, деятельность, абстрактные понятия: work, iron, time. Одно слово может быть обоих типов в зависимости от значения: glass — стекло (U), a glass — стакан (C).

Exercise 14 — Writing: Opinion Essay (110–120 words)

Comment on this quote: “Do what you love and success will follow. Passion is the fuel behind a successful career.”

Suggested structure: Introduction + opinion → Argument 1 and example → Argument 2 and example → Conclusion. Useful links: Firstly, For example, Moreover, However, In conclusion.
0 words
Sample essay (teacher-created)

Many people believe that loving your job is the key to success. I partly agree with this idea because passion can help us work harder and remain motivated.

Firstly, when people enjoy their work, they often want to improve their skills. For example, a software developer who loves technology may spend extra time learning new programming languages. Secondly, passion can help us overcome difficulties. Even after making mistakes, we may continue trying because our goal matters to us.

However, passion alone is not enough. We also need discipline, education and opportunities. In conclusion, I believe that passion is an excellent starting point, but lasting success requires effort and practical skills too.

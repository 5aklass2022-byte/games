I. Reading — 5 points

Our School Life

My name is Anna and I enjoy school. My favourite subject is English because I can communicate with children from other countries. In our classroom, we have a projector and a large screen. We must arrive on time and be polite to our classmates. I always share my books and I never make fun of other pupils. After school, I go to the Arts and Crafts Club. I like painting, but my friend Mark prefers the Science Club. On Wednesdays, we visit the school library. We can check out books with a library card. We have to use headphones if we want to listen to audio books.

Read the text. Choose True or False.

1. Anna likes English because she can communicate with other children.
2. There is a projector in Anna’s classroom.
3. Anna often makes fun of classmates.
4. Mark prefers the Arts and Crafts Club.
5. Pupils have to use headphones for audio books in the library.

V. Writing — 15 points

Describe your favourite afterschool activity and explain why you enjoy it.

Write 60–80 words. Describe the activity, give two reasons with examples and finish with your opinion.

Model: My favourite afterschool activity is the Art Club. I go there every Tuesday with my friends. First, I like painting because I can be creative. For example, I often paint pictures of nature. Second, the club helps me make friends. For example, we work together on colourful posters for our school. Our teacher is friendly and gives us useful advice. I think the Art Club is a wonderful place to learn and have fun.

🏡 Our Family Traditions

Grade 6 · Interactive discovery lesson · 5 screens

Learning goals: I can name family traditions · I can understand a short text · I can describe a tradition.

1. Discover 🔎

Say the tongue twister twice: Betty’s big busy brothers bake bright birthday biscuits.

Explore: Choose the activities that can become a family tradition. Click the cards.

Think: What makes an activity a tradition?

2. Explore words 📚

Read each word, guess its meaning, then reveal it. You can also hear the English word.

Try it: Which word means “made at home”?

3. Read & investigate 📖

AI-generated educational reading, created for this artefact; not a quotation from the textbook.

Peter: My favourite family tradition is going to football matches with my dad. I can play football, too. On Saturdays, we often spend time together and talk about the game.

Silvia: My grandparents live outside the town. At Christmas, I make cards for my family. We visit our grandparents, sing carols and bake homemade cookies. We have many traditions, but making cards is my favourite.

Audio uses your browser's speech synthesis and may depend on installed voices.

Investigate: True or false?

4. Create & reflect ✍️

Write 4–6 sentences about your favourite family tradition. Use the questions: What? Who? When? What do you do? Why is it special?

Self-check:

Your text stays in your browser; it is not sent to a server. The teacher evaluates open writing.

5. Mini-test: Check what you learned ✅

Choose one answer for each question. You will receive a score out of 7 and feedback.

I. Reading — 5 points

A Better Classroom

Our class has a bright classroom with twenty-four chairs, a whiteboard and a computer. There are living plants near the windows. Our teacher says that we must keep the classroom clean and listen to one another. We can ask questions when we do not understand. On Fridays, our class works on a school newspaper. I like this activity because I can write short articles with my friends. After lessons, I sometimes go to the library to find information. I have to return borrowed books after two weeks. I do not have to pay to read books there.

Read the text. Choose True or False.

1. There are living plants near the windows.
2. Pupils must keep the classroom clean.
3. The class makes a newspaper on Mondays.
4. The writer goes to the library for information.
5. The writer has to return borrowed books after two weeks.

V. Writing — 15 points

Describe your ideal classroom and explain why you like it.

Write 60–80 words. Describe two classroom features, give two reasons and include a short conclusion.

Model: My ideal classroom is bright and comfortable. It has large windows, colourful posters and living plants. First, I would like a computer and a screen because they help us learn English. For example, we can watch short educational videos. Second, I want a reading corner because I enjoy books. For example, I can read stories during breaks. I think this classroom would make learning more interesting.

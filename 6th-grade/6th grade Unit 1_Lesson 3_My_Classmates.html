05 · GRAMMAR

CAN

Ability / possibility

I can speak English.
Cows cannot fly.

🇷🇴 abilitate / posibilitate
🇷🇺 способность / возможность

MAY

Permission / possibility

May I take your book?
You may see monkeys at the zoo.

🇷🇴 permisiune / posibilitate
🇷🇺 разрешение / возможность

MUST

Obligation / necessity

We must help each other.

🇷🇴 obligație / necesitate

Exercise 9 · Are you a good classmate? Why?

Use: friendly / unfriendly; supportive / indifferent; kind / rude; helpful / unhelpful; sociable / shy; take turns / interrupt.

My name is … . I live in … and I am a pupil in the … form. I think I am a … classmate because … . I usually … .
My name is Alex. I live in Cahul and I am a pupil in the sixth form. I think I am a good classmate because I am kind and helpful. I listen when my friends speak, and I take turns in conversations. For example, I help my classmates with difficult homework. I also invite classmates who feel lonely to join our games. Sometimes I interrupt people, but I am learning to be more patient. I believe friendship grows when we respect one another.

Checklist: ✓ describe your attitude ✓ explain why ✓ give two examples ✓ use friendly language ✓ check spelling and punctuation

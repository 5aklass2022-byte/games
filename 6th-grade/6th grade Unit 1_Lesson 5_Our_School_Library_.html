GRAMMAR

🧩 Have to / Don’t have to

have to / has to

Necessary: I have to return the book. Ann has to use headphones.

don’t have to / doesn’t have to

Not necessary: I don’t have to buy a book. He doesn’t have to come today.

must not / can’t

Not allowed: You must not eat in the library. ⚠ This is different from don’t have to.

WRITING · EXERCISE 8

✍️ My Sunday

Write what you have to do or don’t have to do on Sundays. Use the textbook prompts: help my mother make breakfast; wash the dishes; call the teacher; wash my pet; play with my little brother/sister; water the flowers; do my homework.

Example: I don’t have to get up early on Sundays.

On Sundays, I have to help my mother make breakfast. I have to water the flowers. I don’t have to call the teacher. I sometimes have to wash the dishes. I have to do my homework, but I don’t have to get up early.

📘 Grammar Focus — Possessive Case

What is it? The possessive case shows that something belongs to someone or describes a relationship: Kate’s dream (the dream of Kate).

Rule 1: Singular noun + ’s. Nick’s father; my mother’s job; the teacher’s book.

Rule 2: Regular plural ending in -s + ’. my parents’ jobs; the students’ books; the girls’ school.

Rule 3: Irregular plural + ’s. children’s toys; men’s shoes; women’s clothes.

Two owners: Ann and Dan’s father = one father shared by both; Ann’s and Dan’s books = separate books belonging to each.

Don’t confuse: it’s = it is; its = belonging to it. My sister’s a doctor can mean My sister is a doctor, not possession.

🇷🇴 Explicație

Cazul posesiv arată apartenența. Singular: ’s (Kate’s book). Plural regulat: ’ (parents’ jobs). Plural neregulat: ’s (children’s toys).

🇷🇺 Объяснение

Притяжательный падеж показывает принадлежность. Единственное число: ’s (Kate’s book). Множественное с окончанием -s: ’ (parents’ jobs). Неправильное множественное: ’s (children’s toys).

✍️ Exercise 9 — Write about Your Parents’ Jobs

Use the clues: your father’s / mother’s job and place of work; what your father / mother does; what he / she thinks about the work.

Model paragraph (fictional)

My mother is a teacher. She works at school. She teaches English and helps her students learn new words. She likes her job because she enjoys working with children. My father is a driver. He works for a transport company. He drives carefully and sometimes works long hours. He thinks his job is interesting but tiring. I am proud of my parents.

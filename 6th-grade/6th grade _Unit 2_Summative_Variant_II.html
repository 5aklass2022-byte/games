A Job That Helps People

On Monday, Daniel visited his aunt, who works as a doctor at a small hospital. She usually starts work at eight o’clock and sometimes works long hours. Daniel wanted to learn about her job because he dreams of helping people. His aunt showed him the waiting room and explained how doctors consult patients. She told him that doctors listen carefully, examine patients and prescribe medicine when necessary. Daniel also met a friendly nurse who helped an elderly patient find the right room. At lunchtime, Daniel and his aunt talked about different professions. He understood that being a doctor is difficult but rewarding. When he returned home, he wrote a short report about the visit for his classmates.

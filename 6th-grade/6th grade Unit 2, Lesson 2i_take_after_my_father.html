🧠 Grammar Academy • Comparisons

Comparative: -er / more ... than

short → shorter than; young → younger than; heavy → heavier than; patient → more patient than.

Equality: as ... as / not as ... as

Nick is as brave as Kevin. (equal) • Allan is not as active as Eddy. (less active)

Superlative: the -est / the most

the tallest, the kindest, the most patient. Irregular: good → better → the best.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăComparativul arată diferențe: taller than = mai înalt decât; more patient than = mai răbdător decât. Egalitatea: as tall as = la fel de înalt ca. Inegalitatea: not as active as = nu la fel de activ ca. Superlativul: the tallest / the most patient = cel mai înalt / cel mai răbdător.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиСравнительная степень: taller than = выше, чем; more patient than = терпеливее, чем. Равенство: as tall as = такой же высокий, как. Неравенство: not as active as = не такой активный, как. Превосходная степень: the tallest / the most patient = самый высокий / самый терпеливый.

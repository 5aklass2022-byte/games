A Family Celebration

Last Saturday, Emma and her family celebrated her grandmother’s birthday. Emma’s parents had prepared a surprise party in their garden. Her father baked a chocolate cake, and her mother decorated the table with flowers. Emma invited her cousins and helped her little brother make a birthday card. At four o’clock, Grandma arrived. She was surprised to see everyone together. The children sang a birthday song, and Grandma told them stories about family traditions. After the party, Emma helped wash the dishes. She was tired but happy because the whole family had spent a wonderful afternoon together.

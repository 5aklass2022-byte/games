🎤 Oral assessment

1Can you say why English is important?

Say 3–4 sentences aloud. Use the prompts if you need help.

English helps me… · I can… · It is useful because…

English is important because people use it around the world. It helps me communicate with new friends. I can read books and watch videos in English. I want to learn it better.

2Can you describe the classroom in the picture?

Look at the picture on page 16 or describe your classroom.

There is / There are… · On the left… · Near the window… · I like…

Our classroom is bright and comfortable. There are desks and chairs. There is a board in front of the class. We have books and posters. I like studying here.

3Can you talk about your best classmate?

Talk about a helpful classmate. Give examples.

My best classmate is… · He / She is… · We… · I appreciate…

My best classmate is friendly and kind. She helps me when I do not understand a task. We often work together. She is respectful and never calls anyone bad names.

✍️ Written assessment

4Can you write four polite imperative sentences?

Write four polite instructions or requests.

1. Please open your books.
2. Speak quietly, please.
3. Don't run in the library, please.
4. Please help your classmate.

6Can you write about your favourite school activity?

Write 5–7 sentences. Explain what you do, when you do it and why you enjoy it.

My favourite school activity is… · We meet… · I enjoy it because… · It helps me…

0 words

My favourite school activity is the English Speaking Club. We meet every Thursday after classes. We play word games and practise speaking. I like working with my friends. Our teacher gives us interesting activities. The club helps me feel more confident in English.

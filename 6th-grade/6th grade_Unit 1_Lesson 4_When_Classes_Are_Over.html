GRAMMAR

🧩 Comparison · as … as

Needlework is as interesting as painting.
Reciting is not as difficult as writing poetry.

🇷🇴 Română

as + adjectiv + as exprimă egalitatea: la fel de … ca. not as … as exprimă diferența: nu la fel de … ca.

🇷🇺 Русский

as + прилагательное + as означает «такой же … как». not as … as означает «не такой … как».

✍️ Exercise 10 · My favourite afterschool activity

Write about the afterschool activity you enjoy most. Include what you do, when you do it, who joins you and why you like it.

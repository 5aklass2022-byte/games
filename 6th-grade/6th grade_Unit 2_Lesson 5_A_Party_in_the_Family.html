📘 Grammar Focus — Past Simple

What is the Past Simple?

We use the Past Simple for completed actions and events in the past: yesterday, last Saturday, two days ago, in 2020.

Affirmative: I played a game. / She made a cake.
Negative: I did not (didn’t) play. / She didn’t make a cake.
Question: Did you play? / Did she make a cake?

Regular verbs: play → played, invite → invited, recite → recited. For a consonant + y: study → studied. Some short verbs double the final consonant: stop → stopped.

Irregular verbs: have → had, make → made, eat → ate, sing → sang, be → was/were.

Important: After did / didn’t, use the base form: Did you go? (not Did you went?).

🇷🇴 Explicație

Past Simple descrie acțiuni încheiate în trecut. Verbele regulate primesc -ed; cele neregulate au forme speciale. În întrebări și negații folosim did / didn’t + forma de bază.

🇷🇺 Объяснение

Past Simple обозначает завершённые действия в прошлом. Правильные глаголы получают окончание -ed, неправильные имеют особые формы. В вопросах и отрицаниях используется did / didn’t + начальная форма глагола.

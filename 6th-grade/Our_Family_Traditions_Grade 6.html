🏡 Our Family Traditions

Explore, listen, discover, practise and check your progress.

Lesson objectives — by the end of the lesson, you will be able:
  1. To identify and use words related to family traditions.
  2. To understand a short text and audio about family traditions.
  3. To describe a favourite family tradition using simple sentences.

1. Discover: What is a family tradition? 🔎

Look at the activities. Select those that can become family traditions. There may be more than one correct answer.

A family tradition is an activity a family repeats and values, for example baking together every Christmas or visiting grandparents every Sunday.
🇷🇴 O tradiție de familie este o activitate repetată și prețuită de familie.
🇷🇺 Семейная традиция — это занятие, которое семья регулярно повторяет и ценит.

Peter’s family

My favourite family tradition is going to football matches with my dad. I can play football, too. We often spend time together and talk about the game. These moments are important to me.

Silvia’s family

My grandparents live outside the town. At Christmas, I make cards for my family. We visit our grandparents, sing carols and bake homemade cookies. We have many family traditions, but making Christmas cards is my favourite.

4. Create: My favourite family tradition ✍️

Write 4–6 sentences. Answer: What tradition? Who? When? What do you do? Why do you like it?

Model: Our favourite family tradition is baking cookies at Christmas. We started it with my grandma. We make cookies together and sing carols. I like this tradition because we spend time together.

🇷🇴 Tradiția noastră preferată este să coacem biscuiți de Crăciun. Îmi place deoarece petrecem timp împreună.

🇷🇺 Наша любимая традиция — печь печенье на Рождество. Мне это нравится, потому что мы проводим время вместе.

🚲 Read and listen: Family Cycling

Poem from the original PowerPoint presentation. Read it first, then open the Google Forms exercise below.

Family cycling is so exciting,
It is so funny and so uniting!
Traditions are those special times
That bring families together.
We are so happy to ride bikes together
When it is sunny and in good weather!
🇷🇴 Sensul poeziei: Mersul cu bicicleta împreună este o tradiție plăcută care unește familia, mai ales pe vreme frumoasă.
🇷🇺 Смысл стихотворения: Совместные велосипедные прогулки — приятная семейная традиция, которая объединяет близких в хорошую погоду.

Think before opening the form: What family tradition is described? Why does the family enjoy it?

📘 Grammar Focus — Time Clauses

What is a time clause?

A time clause tells us when something happens. It can refer to the future even though its verb is in the present tense.

Structure: Main clause (will + verb) + time conjunction + time clause (present simple).

✅ I will call you when I get home.
❌ I will call you when I will get home.

Conjunctions: when, after, before, as soon as, until, while, in case, by the time.

Examples:
She will leave after she finishes work.
We won't start until everybody arrives.
As soon as you press the button, the door will open.
I will be studying while you are travelling.

Important: Present simple is common for future time clauses; present perfect can emphasize completion (“I'll call after I have finished”). While often introduces an ongoing action and may use present continuous.

Commas: When the time clause comes first, normally use a comma: “When she arrives, we will eat.”

Memory trick: Future meaning does NOT automatically mean “will” in both clauses.

🇷🇴 Explicație

Propozițiile temporale arată când se petrece o acțiune. După when, after, before, as soon as, until folosim de obicei Present Simple pentru viitor, iar în propoziția principală putem folosi will. Exemplu: I will eat when I get home. Nu: when I will get home.

🇷🇺 Объяснение

Придаточное времени сообщает, когда произойдёт действие. После when, after, before, as soon as, until для будущего обычно употребляется Present Simple, а в главном предложении — will. Пример: I will eat when I get home. Не: when I will get home.

Exercise 12 — Food trends

Some of the most recent food trends include the vegetarian, vegan and raw vegan diets. What is the difference between them? What are the benefits and the risks associated with these diets?

Discussion support (additional information):

Vegetarian: excludes meat and fish; some vegetarians consume eggs and dairy.
Vegan: excludes animal-derived foods.
Raw vegan: vegan diet emphasizing uncooked or minimally heated plant foods.

Consider: dietary variety, protein, vitamin B12, iron, calcium, and adequate energy. A vegan diet needs reliable vitamin B12 sources; restrictive raw vegan diets may make adequate nutrition harder. Avoid presenting one diet as automatically healthier for everyone.

Additional model — Statement 1

Is a vegetarian diet always healthier? In my opinion, it depends on what people eat every day. Firstly, a vegetarian diet can include plenty of vegetables, fruit and beans, which provide useful nutrients. For example, a balanced lunch with lentils and vegetables can be nutritious. However, some vegetarian meals contain too much sugar or salt. Secondly, people who eat meat can also make healthy choices. For instance, they can choose fish, vegetables and whole grains. To conclude, I believe that a balanced diet matters more than a simple label. Everyone should pay attention to food quality and variety.

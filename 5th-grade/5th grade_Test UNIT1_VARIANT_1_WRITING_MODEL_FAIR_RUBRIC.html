Emma's Family and Home

My name is Emma, and I am eleven years old. I live with my parents and my younger brother, Ben. Ben is nine. We have got a small house with a lovely garden. There are two bedrooms upstairs. My bedroom is next to Ben’s room. Downstairs, there is a kitchen and a living room. The living room is next to the kitchen.

In our family, everyone helps at home. I feed our dog every morning and tidy my bedroom on Saturdays. Ben waters the flowers after school. Dad cooks dinner on Sundays, and Mum does the laundry. My mother is a doctor at a hospital. My father is a bus driver. They work hard, but we always have time to talk and play together. I am good at English and I want to be a teacher when I grow up.

VI. Writing · My Family and Our Home

Write 40–50 words about your family and your home. Include:

  1. who is in your family;
  2. what your home is like (rooms or garden);
  3. two household chores you or your family members do;
  4. one job a family member has or a job you want in the future.

Writing tip: Use short, clear sentences. You can use have got, there is/are, Present Simple and will if appropriate.

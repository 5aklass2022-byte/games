🧩 Grammar — Present Simple: BE

👀 LOOK → CHOOSE → SPEAK
👤 IAMI am eleven.
👩 He · She · ItISShe is my friend.
👥 You · We · TheyAREThey are classmates.

❓ Questions: Are you eleven? / Is she a pupil?

❌ Negatives: I am not · She isn't · They aren't.

🇷🇴 Explicație în română

am cu I, is cu he/she/it, are cu you/we/they. La întrebări punem verbul înaintea persoanei; pentru negație adăugăm not.

🇷🇺 Объяснение на русском

am с I, is с he/she/it, are с you/we/they. В вопросе ставим глагол перед подлежащим, в отрицании добавляем not.

🎯 Useful phrases — good at / fond of

be good at + -ing

She is good at playing badminton.

be fond of + -ing

He is fond of reading.

🇷🇴 Română

good at — se pricepe la; fond of — îi place mult. După aceste expresii, verbul are forma -ing.

🇷🇺 Русский

good at — хорошо умеет; fond of — увлекается. После выражений используется глагол с окончанием -ing.

💡 Model: “Hi!” ends with an exclamation mark.

✍️ Punctuation tips — from the textbook

1. Exclamation mark (!) after an exclamation.
2. Capital letter at the start of a sentence.
3. Comma (,) in a list.
4. Full stop (.) at the end of a sentence.
5. Question mark (?) after a question.

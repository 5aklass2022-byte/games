🧩 Grammar Guide • Present Continuous

We use it for actions happening NOW.

1 • I

I am reading.

I + am + verb-ing

2 • He / She / It

She is writing.

He / She / It + is + verb-ing

3 • We / You / They

We are playing.

We / You / They + are + verb-ing

Questions: Are you listening? → Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Is he wearing jeans? → Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t.
Negative: She is not sleeping. / They are not running.
🇷🇴 Explicație în românăFolosim Present Continuous pentru acțiuni care se desfășoară acum. Formă: am / is / are + verb + -ing. Exemplu: She is reading. = Ea citește acum. La întrebări, am/is/are se pune înaintea subiectului.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиPresent Continuous обозначает действие, происходящее сейчас. Формула: am / is / are + глагол с -ing. Например: She is reading. — Она сейчас читает. В вопросах am/is/are ставится перед подлежащим.

Daniel's Busy Family

My name is Daniel, and I am eleven years old. I live with my mum, dad and older sister, Lucy. Lucy is thirteen. We have got a comfortable flat on the second floor. There are three bedrooms, a kitchen and a living room. There is a small balcony next to the living room. My room is opposite Lucy’s bedroom.

We all do household chores. I make my bed every morning and take the rubbish out after school. Lucy washes the dishes in the evening. Mum waters the flowers on the balcony, and Dad cleans the kitchen on Saturdays. My mother is a nurse at a hospital. My father is a teacher at a school. I am fond of drawing and I am good at maths. When I grow up, I want to be an engineer. On Sundays, our family likes walking in the park together.

VI. Writing · My Family and My Daily Life

Write 40–50 words about your family and your daily life. Include:

  1. who is in your family;
  2. one or two rooms in your home;
  3. two household chores you or your family members do;
  4. a family member’s job and what you want to be in the future.

Writing tip: Use short, clear sentences. You can use have got, there is/are, Present Simple and will if appropriate.

🧩 Grammar Adventure: HAVE GOT / HAS GOT

Look 👀 → Understand 💡 → Try 🎯

✨ We use have got / has got to talk about our family, things and appearance.
1

👨‍👩‍👧 Who has got what?

I · YOU · WE · THEYHAVE GOT👨‍👩‍👧 We have got a big family.
HE · SHE · ITHAS GOT👧 She has got a sister.
Regula: I, you, we, they → have got; he, she, it → has got. „We have got a big family” = Avem o familie mare. „She has got a sister” = Ea are o soră.
Правило: I, you, we, they → have got; he, she, it → has got. «We have got a big family» = У нас большая семья. «She has got a sister» = У неё есть сестра.
2

❌ Say NO: haven't got / hasn't got

They have got a cat.➡️They haven't got a cat.
He has got a dog.➡️He hasn't got a dog.
La negativ: have not got = haven't got; has not got = hasn't got. Înseamnă „nu are / nu au”.
В отрицании: have not got = haven't got; has not got = hasn't got. Значение: «нет / не имеет».
3

❓ Ask a question: move HAVE / HAS to the front

She has got a cousin.➡️Has she got a cousin?
💬 Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.
They have got a dog.➡️Have they got a dog?
💬 Yes, they have. / No, they haven't.
Pentru întrebări, punem Have / Has înaintea persoanei. Răspunsuri scurte: Yes, she has / No, she hasn't.
В вопросе ставим Have / Has перед подлежащим. Краткий ответ: Yes, she has / No, she hasn't.
🧠 Remember: She has got blue eyes. ✅   She have got blue eyes. ❌

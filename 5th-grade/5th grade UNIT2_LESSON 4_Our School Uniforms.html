🧠 Grammar • Singular and plural nouns

Learn the rule before Exercise 7.

👕 ONE

a shirt
a tie
a dress
Singular = one item

👕👕 MORE THAN ONE

two shirts
three ties
two dresses
Plural = two or more items

👖 ALWAYS PLURAL

jeans • trousers • shorts • tights
Say: My trousers are blue.

Singular = un singur obiect: a shirt (o cămașă). Plural = mai multe: shirts (cămăși). Jeans, trousers, shorts, tights se folosesc la plural: My jeans are blue.
Единственное число — один предмет: a shirt (рубашка). Множественное — несколько: shirts (рубашки). Jeans, trousers, shorts, tights употребляются во множественном числе: My jeans are blue.

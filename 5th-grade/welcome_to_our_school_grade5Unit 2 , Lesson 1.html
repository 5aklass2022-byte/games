🧠 Grammar Academy: Past Simple

1. Past Simple — verb to be

We use was / were to talk about the past. I / he / she / it → was; you / we / they → were.

Positive: I was at school yesterday. / They were happy.

Negative: I wasn't late. / They weren't tired.

Questions: Were you at school? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăPast Simple exprimă acțiuni și stări din trecut. Was se folosește cu I/he/she/it; were cu you/we/they. La negație: wasn't / weren't.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиPast Simple обозначает действия и состояния в прошлом. Was употребляется с I/he/she/it, were — с you/we/they. Отрицание: wasn't / weren't.

2. Past Simple — regular verbs

Add -ed to most regular verbs: play → played; wash → washed; help → helped. After consonant + y: study → studied.

Questions: Did you play chess? Negatives: I didn't play chess. After did / didn't, use the base verb, not played.

Remember irregular forms: can → could; have → had; do → did; go → went; see → saw.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăVerbele regulate primesc terminația -ed la Past Simple. În întrebări folosim Did + subiect + verb la forma de bază; în negații didn't + verb la forma de bază.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиПравильные глаголы в Past Simple получают окончание -ed. В вопросах: Did + подлежащее + начальная форма глагола; в отрицаниях: didn't + начальная форма глагола.

🔊 Pronunciation Guide — /uː/ and /ʊ/

/uː/ — long sound: do, cool, pool, too, who, soon, school, room, tooth, you.

/ʊ/ — short sound: good, book, cook, foot, football, look, could.

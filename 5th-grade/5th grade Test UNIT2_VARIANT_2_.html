A Busy Week at School

I am Daniel, and I am ten. My school is small but comfortable. It has a tidy classroom, a canteen, a library and a playground. My favourite subject is Geography because I like learning about countries. My classmate Eva enjoys Art. She is friendly and sociable, and she often helps other pupils. We have lessons from nine o’clock until two o’clock. Our school uniform is a blue shirt and grey trousers or a skirt. In my free time, I enjoy playing chess and watching cartoons. Last weekend, I visited my grandparents. On Saturday, we cooked lunch together. On Sunday, I rode my bike in the park. It was fun, but I was tired in the evening.

VI. Writing · My School and My Weekend

Write 40–50 words about your family and your daily life. Include:

  1. describe two places or facilities at your school;
  2. say which lessons you like and why;
  3. describe a classmate and your favourite free-time activity;
  4. say what you did last weekend.

Writing tip: Use clear sentences. You can use Present Simple, like/enjoy + -ing and Past Simple.

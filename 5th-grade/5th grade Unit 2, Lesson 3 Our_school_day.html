🧠 Grammar Academy — Prepositions of Time

INONAT
months: in April
seasons: in winter
parts of the day: in the morning		days: on Monday
days of the week: on Tuesday		clock times: at 5 o'clock
at 2:30
fixed phrase: at night
Remember: in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening — but at night!
🇷🇴 Explicație în românăIN se folosește cu lunile, anotimpurile și părțile zilei (in May, in winter, in the morning). ON se folosește cu zilele săptămânii (on Monday). AT se folosește cu orele exacte (at 8:30) și în expresia at night.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиIN употребляется с месяцами, временами года и частями дня (in May, in winter, in the morning). ON — с днями недели (on Monday). AT — с точным временем (at 8:30), а также в выражении at night.

🔊 Pronunciation — Silent Letters

Some letters are written but not pronounced. Listen and repeat.

Silent letterExamples
kknife, know, knock
lwalk, talk, half
hrhyme, ghost, why, when (pronunciation varies; h is silent in rhyme and ghost)
wanswer, whole, who
cexcellent, excited (the c is not always fully silent; listen carefully)
gheight, right, fight
A careful distinction: when usually has a pronounced /w/ and /h/ is silent in most accents; excellent and excited have pronounced consonant sounds represented by the spelling xc.

⏰ Daily Routine — Adverbs of Frequency

Always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → never (0%).

They usually go before the main verb: I usually wake up at 7 o'clock. They go after be: I am always happy.

🇷🇴 Explicațiealways — întotdeauna; usually — de obicei; often — adesea; sometimes — uneori; never — niciodată. În general se plasează înaintea verbului principal.
🇷🇺 Объяснениеalways — всегда; usually — обычно; often — часто; sometimes — иногда; never — никогда. Обычно ставятся перед смысловым глаголом.

✍️ Writing Studio — My School Day

Write 6–9 sentences about your own school day. Use in / on / at, times, school subjects, breaks, and frequency adverbs.

Model: I usually wake up at seven o’clock. I have breakfast in the morning. I go to school at eight o’clock. On Monday, I have English and maths. I often play with my friends during the break. We have lunch at school. I go home in the afternoon.

Automatic checks assess length and selected features, not full grammar accuracy. Your teacher can check the final text.

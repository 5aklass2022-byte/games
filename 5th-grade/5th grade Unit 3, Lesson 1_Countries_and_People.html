Remember! Country → Nationality → Language

Country • Nationality • Language

A country is a place. Nationality tells us what nation a person belongs to. A language is what people speak. Use from + country, be + nationality, speak + language. All three begin with capital letters.

Țara este un loc. Naționalitatea arată apartenența la o națiune. Limba este ceea ce vorbim. Folosim from + țară, be + naționalitate, speak + limbă. Toate se scriu cu majusculă.

Страна — место. Национальность обозначает принадлежность к нации. Язык — то, на чём говорят. Используем from + страна, be + национальность, speak + язык. Все эти названия пишутся с заглавной буквы.

CountryWhere are you from?I am from France.
NationalityWhat nationality are you?I am French.
LanguageWhat language do you speak?I speak French.

Grammar Focus · Articles with countries

Articles with country names

Most country names take no article: in France, from Italy. Use the with names such as the United Kingdom and the United States (the UK, the USA). Do not use the before nationality or language names in these examples.

Majoritatea numelor de țări nu au articol: in France, from Italy. Folosim the cu the United Kingdom și the United States (the UK, the USA). Nu folosim the înaintea naționalității sau limbii în aceste exemple.

Большинство названий стран употребляются без артикля: in France, from Italy. Используем the с the United Kingdom и the United States (the UK, the USA). В этих примерах перед национальностью и языком артикль the не нужен.

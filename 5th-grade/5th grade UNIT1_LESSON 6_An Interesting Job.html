👂 Pronunciation Guide: job endings

Listen and notice how the final -s / -es sounds change.

Tip: /s/ is voiceless, /z/ makes your throat vibrate, /ɪz/ adds an extra syllable. The spelling alone doesn't tell you the sound.

Terminația -s se pronunță /s/ după sunete surde, /z/ după sunete sonore, iar /ɪz/ după sunete precum /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/.

Окончание -s произносится /s/ после глухих звуков, /z/ после звонких, /ɪz/ после свистящих и шипящих звуков.

Grammar Guide — before Exercise 6

🧠 Present Simple: he / she / it

One simple idea: when we describe what one person does regularly, the verb usually changes.

👥 I / you / we / they

I drive a bus.
They teach pupils.

👤 He / she / it

He drives a bus.
She teaches pupils.

❓ Questions

Does she work every day?
Yes, she does.
No, she doesn't.

How do we add the ending?

Most verbs → +s

work → works
like → likes

-ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o → +es

wash → washes
go → goes
teach → teaches

Consonant + y → -ies

study → studies
carry → carries

Remember: After does, use the base form: Does she work? (not “works”).

La persoana a III-a singular (he/she/it), în Present Simple adăugăm de obicei -s. După -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o adăugăm -es. Dacă verbul se termină în consoană + y, schimbăm y în -ies. În întrebări folosim does și forma de bază a verbului.
В Present Simple с he/she/it к глаголу обычно добавляем -s. После -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o добавляем -es. Если глагол оканчивается на согласную + y, меняем y на -ies. В вопросах используем does и начальную форму глагола.

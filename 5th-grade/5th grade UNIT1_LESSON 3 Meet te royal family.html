✨ Grammar before Exercise 7 · Possessive Case of Nouns

We use ’s to say who someone or something belongs to.

👤 One person → ’s

Philip + ’s → Philip’s

Prince Philip’s hobby is fishing.

🇷🇴 Adăugăm ’s pentru a arăta posesia: hobby-ul lui Philip.
🇷🇺 Добавляем ’s, чтобы показать принадлежность: хобби Филиппа.

👥 Plural ending in -s → ’

boys + ’ → boys’

Prince Philip is the boys’ grandfather.

🇷🇴 Dacă pluralul se termină în -s, adăugăm numai apostroful.
🇷🇺 Если множественное число заканчивается на -s, ставим только апостроф.
Remember: the Queen’s birthday = the birthday of the Queen.

Exercise 7 · Make possessive nouns

Use the words in brackets. Textbook model: Prince ___ hobby is fishing. (Philip) → Prince Philip’s hobby is fishing.

1. The ___ birthday is in April. (Queen)
2. Her ___ name is Ann. (daughter)
3. The Queen’s children share their ___ love of outdoor life. (parents)
4. The eldest ___ hobbies are fishing and shooting. (son)
5. ___ family is larger than Henry’s. (William)
6. Archie is ___ son. (Henry)
7. George, Charlotte and Louis are ___ cousins. (Archie)

Alex

🧠 Grammar Academy — Comparison

More / Less + adjective + than

Use more ... than to compare two things: Maths is more interesting than history.

Use less ... than to say something has a lower degree of a quality: History is less difficult than maths.

The most / The least + adjective

Use the most for the highest degree: Science is the most useful subject.

Use the least for the lowest degree: For me, music is the least difficult subject.

⚠️ For short adjectives, English often uses -er / -est (e.g., hard → harder → the hardest). This lesson focuses on more, less, the most, the least with adjectives such as interesting, difficult, useful and exciting.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăMore ... than = mai ... decât; less ... than = mai puțin ... decât; the most = cel mai / cea mai; the least = cel mai puțin / cea mai puțin. Exemplu: Maths is more interesting than history. = Matematica este mai interesantă decât istoria.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиMore ... than = более ... чем; less ... than = менее ... чем; the most = самый / наиболее; the least = наименее. Пример: Maths is more interesting than history. = Математика интереснее истории.

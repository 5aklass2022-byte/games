✨ Grammar before Exercise 7 · There is / There are

Look at the picture in your mind: one thing or more than one?

🪑

ONE → THERE IS

There is a chair.

🪑 🪑

MANY → THERE ARE

There are two chairs.

❌ Negative

There isn’t a garden.

There aren’t any chairs.

❓ Questions

Is there a lamp? — Yes, there is.

Are there chairs? — No, there aren’t.

There is = există / este (un singur obiect). There are = există / sunt (mai multe obiecte). Pentru negație: isn't / aren't. În întrebare, punem Is / Are la început.
There is — «есть / находится» (один предмет). There are — «есть / находятся» (несколько предметов). Отрицание: isn't / aren't. В вопросе Is / Are ставим в начало.

✍️ Exercise 8 · Describe your house or flat

Follow the textbook plan: 1. Introduce your home. 2. Describe the furniture. 3. Explain why you like it.

Writing tip: Use and to connect similar ideas and but for contrasting ideas.
There is a bedroom and a living room.
There is a bed but there are no armchairs.
Model: I live in a small flat with my family. There are two bedrooms, a kitchen, a bathroom and a living room. There is a sofa and a table in the living room. There are some lamps, but there isn’t a big garden. I like my home because it is cosy and comfortable.

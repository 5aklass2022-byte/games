· Grammar Focus · Reflexive pronouns

Reflexive pronouns

A reflexive pronoun refers back to the subject when the subject and object are the same person. Use -self for singular and -selves for plural.

I hurt myself. They enjoyed themselves.

Use by + reflexive pronoun to mean alone. Do not say “I hurt himself”.
Un pronume reflexiv se referă la subiect când subiectul și complementul desemnează aceeași persoană. Folosim -self la singular și -selves la plural.

I hurt myself. They enjoyed themselves.

by + pronume reflexiv înseamnă singur. Nu spunem “I hurt himself”.
Возвратное местоимение относится к подлежащему, когда субъект и объект — одно лицо. -self — единственное число, -selves — множественное.

I hurt myself. They enjoyed themselves.

by + возвратное местоимение означает самостоятельно. Нельзя говорить “I hurt himself”.

Three Reasons to Visit the Grand Canyon

The Grand Canyon is a famous natural landmark located in Arizona, USA. There are three reasons why it is worth visiting. First, visitors can enjoy the picturesque scenery and admire the colourful rocks. Second, they can discover how the Colorado River carved this vast canyon over millions of years. Finally, tourists can explore numerous viewpoints, take beautiful photographs and learn about the geological history of the Earth. The Grand Canyon is an enchanting destination for anyone who loves nature and adventure.

A School Exchange to Remember

Last autumn, Elena, a fourteen-year-old student from Moldova, joined a school exchange programme in Scotland. On her first day, she was surprised to see that most pupils wore dark blue uniforms. Elena's host sister, Sophie, explained that the uniform helped students feel part of the school community. Classes started at nine o'clock, and students usually arrived early. After lessons, Sophie took Elena to the drama club. They rehearsed a short play for the school's annual celebration. On Wednesday, the class visited an unusual outdoor school where children learned in a forest. The teachers said that outdoor activities encouraged teamwork and independence. Elena had never studied outside before, but she enjoyed the experience. At the end of the week, Elena wrote a blog post about Scottish school traditions. She said that the exchange had changed her opinion of school uniforms and extracurricular activities. She hoped to invite Sophie to Moldova the following spring.

VI. Writing — 10 points

Write an email (80–100 words) to a friend about a school exchange you experienced or would like to join. Describe school traditions, uniforms, an after-school activity, and one difference between schools.

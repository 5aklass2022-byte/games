Reading text

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Daniel is a fifteen-year-old pupil who moved to a new school in England last year. At first, he worried about the strict dress code and the unfamiliar timetable. His classmates wore grey trousers, white shirts and green blazers. However, he soon discovered that the school offered many interesting activities. On Mondays, Daniel attended the robotics club, and on Thursdays he helped organise the school newspaper. One day, his class read about unusual schools around the world. Daniel was especially interested in a school in Bangladesh where lessons took place on boats. He thought it was a creative solution for children who could not easily reach an ordinary school. His English teacher asked the class to prepare an infographic about innovative education. Daniel worked with two classmates and completed the project before the deadline. Their teacher said that the infographic was clear and informative. Later, Daniel told his parents that he had become more confident. He also explained that school traditions could bring students together, even when they came from different countries.

My Ideal School My ideal school would be a friendly place where everyone feels welcome. Students could wear comfortable uniforms in different colours. After classes, they could choose a drama club, a sports team or a robotics club. I would also like an outdoor classroom in a garden because fresh air helps us concentrate. Every year, our school would organise a festival where students present their projects and celebrate different cultures. I think these traditions would help classmates work together and respect one another. An ideal school should make learning interesting, practical and enjoyable for everyone.

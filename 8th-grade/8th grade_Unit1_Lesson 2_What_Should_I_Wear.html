📝 Exercise 12 · School Newspaper Article

Write 80–100 words proposing a school uniform for the “Best School Uniform” contest. Include a title, introduction, design details and reasons.

Our New School Uniform

Our school council wants a new uniform, and I have an idea that is both comfortable and practical. I suggest a navy-blue blazer with our school badge, a white shirt and dark trousers or a skirt. Students could also wear a soft cardigan in winter. The clothes should be affordable and easy to wash. I believe this uniform would help students feel part of one community. It would also save time every morning because we would not need to choose an outfit. Our uniform should show school pride without limiting personal comfort.

🧠 Grammar Focus · Time connectors

🌐 RO / RU

AFTER — later than an event: After school, I play football.

BEFORE — earlier than an event: Wash your hands before lunch.

WHILE — two actions happen at the same time: I listened while she was speaking.

UNTIL / TILL — up to a point in time: Wait until five o’clock.

AS SOON AS — immediately when: Call me as soon as you arrive.

IN CASE — as a precaution, because something might happen: Take an umbrella in case it rains.

⚠️ Important: In future-time clauses, use Present Simple: I will call you as soon as I arrive (not “will arrive”).

EXERCISE 12

✍️ My Dream School · Photo Essay

🌐 RO / RU

Write 80–100 words about your dream school and explain what the photos would show.

Suggested structure:

1. Introduction: name and location of the school.
2. Photo 1: classrooms and facilities.
3. Photo 2: lessons, clubs and activities.
4. Conclusion: why you would like to study there.

My dream school would be near a green park. In the first photo, you can see bright classrooms with comfortable desks and modern technology. The students learn science, languages and art in small groups. In the second photo, our class is working in a beautiful school garden. We also have music, robotics and sports clubs after lessons. The school canteen serves healthy food, and everyone can choose a meal. Teachers encourage us to ask questions and help one another. I would love this school because learning would be exciting, creative and friendly every day.

🧠 Grammar Academy — Prepositions

1. Verb / adjective + preposition

rely on someone · believe in something · approve of something · arrive in a city · worry about something · take after someone · look after someone.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăUnele verbe și adjective se folosesc cu prepoziții fixe: rely on = a se baza pe; believe in = a crede în; approve of = a aproba; arrive in = a ajunge într-un oraș; worry about = a-și face griji pentru; take after = a semăna cu; look after = a avea grijă de.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиНекоторые глаголы требуют определённых предлогов: rely on = полагаться на; believe in = верить в; approve of = одобрять; arrive in = прибывать в город; worry about = беспокоиться о; take after = быть похожим на; look after = заботиться о.

2. Phrasal verbs with PLAY

play back = replay a recording · play against = compete with · play up = emphasize · play around = waste time / act playfully · play down = make something seem less important.

🇷🇴 Explicație în românăplay back = a reda o înregistrare; play against = a juca împotriva; play up = a scoate în evidență; play around = a pierde vremea / a se prosti; play down = a minimaliza.
🇷🇺 Объяснение по-русскиplay back = воспроизводить запись; play against = играть против; play up = подчёркивать; play around = дурачиться; play down = преуменьшать.

✍️ Writing Studio — Exercise 12

Task: Choose a famous athlete you admire. Write an 80–100-word photo essay explaining why this person is your role model.

Writing plan:
  1. Title and athlete's name.
  2. Introduction: sport and nationality.
  3. Achievements and challenges.
  4. Character traits and life lessons.
  5. Conclusion: why you admire this athlete.
Use linking words: firstly, moreover, although, because, therefore, finally. Include a photo if available.
📝 Model essay (about 90 words)

My Role Model: Nadia Comăneci 🤸‍♀️

Nadia Comăneci is a famous Romanian gymnast and an inspiring role model. She became the first gymnast to receive a perfect score of ten at the Olympic Games. Her success required years of hard training, courage and discipline. Although gymnastics is difficult, Nadia never stopped believing in her abilities. I admire her because she is talented, determined and hardworking. Her achievements teach me that success is not only about winning medals. It is also about patience and perseverance. Nadia inspires young people to set goals, practise regularly and never give up on their dreams.

Automatic checking assesses word count and structure; your teacher assesses grammar, originality and accuracy.
